## T2 — Account-locked email
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Expected context | Emails to users go through `pending_emails` (`PendingEmail`, `PendingEmailPurpose`: PasswordReset = 1, Verification = 2) and `PendingEmailWorker`, which runs each processor on its own each pass. Follow `PasswordResetEmailProcessor`: claim by conditional update, retries after 1, 2, 4, 8 min, failed after the 5th, failed rows purged after 7 days, at most once after a crash, logs without email address. Templates: embedded HTML in `Templates/` filled by a class in `Services/` (see `InvitationEmailTemplate`), sent with `IEmailService.SendEmailAsync`, values HTML-encoded. Verification and reset emails are English only |
| Expected result | In the same save as the whole-account lock from T1 (only the request that won the conditional update), one `pending_emails` row with the new purpose `AccountLocked` (= 3) is queued for the account's email. A per-address lock queues nothing. A new processor sends it: subject and body in English, first name, "someone tried to sign in to your account several times with a wrong password; sign-in is paused for 15 minutes; if it was not you, reset your password". The email holds no code, no link and no IP address. Unknown account at send time: row removed, nothing sent. At most one account-locked email per account per 24 hours: the time the last lock email was queued is stored on the user as `AccountLockedEmailAt` (new column `users.account_locked_email_at`, own migration; the tests read it by that property name), so it survives the queue row being deleted after sending. A repeat whole-account lock within 24 hours of it queues nothing; one more than 24 hours later queues a new email and updates it (spec assumption, Gate 2). Login's answer and timing do not depend on the email: nothing is sent during the request |
| Outcome | #4: unit tests show one queued email on the whole-account lock, none on an address lock, none for attempts during the lock, and an email body with no code or link; an endpoint test with the worker running shows exactly one email sent for one account lock |
