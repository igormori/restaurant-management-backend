## T4 — Business day calculator
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Expected context | No business-day logic exists. Services depend on interfaces (architecture skill, Section 5). Moments are stored in UTC (Coordinated Universal Time). Built-in `TimeZoneInfo` resolves IANA (Internet Assigned Numbers Authority) names on Linux and macOS; the API image is Debian-based and ships time zone data. No consumer exists yet: sales arrive in Phase 1. |
| Expected result | A business day calculator in the Organization module (interface plus implementation, no database access) takes a UTC moment, an IANA time zone name and a cutoff time of day, and returns the business date: the local date of that moment in that zone, minus one day when the local time is before the cutoff. A moment exactly at the cutoff belongs to the same day. An unknown time zone name is rejected with an argument error. Daylight saving changes are handled by `TimeZoneInfo`. |
| Outcome | #7 — with cutoff 04:00, 02:30 local on 15 Oct gives 14 Oct for `America/Sao_Paulo` (05:30 UTC) and for `America/Toronto` (06:30 UTC); 04:00 local gives 15 Oct. |
